\index{requirements}

The requirements follow from the project description, the teaching
notebooks~A and~B, the AAS6 simulation chain and the decisions of
chapter~\ref{ch:basic-design}. Every requirement has an identifier;
tests and chapters refer to it. \emph{Phase} is the phase of the
development plan in which the requirement is implemented; \emph{Status}:
done, open.

\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Sky}}\\
F-SKY-1 & Sky model of a field centre and point, Gaussian and disc
  components (uniform and limb-brightened). & 2 & done \\
F-SKY-2 & Sky models are read and written in the JSON format of AAS6

F-SKY-3 & Analytic visibility of every component. & 2 & done \\
F-SKY-4 & Maps of a sky model (full field and zoom). & 2 & done \\
F-SKY-5 & Spectrum per component: continuum (spectral index) and
  spectral lines (HI, OH) for several frequency channels. & 2 & open \\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Instrument}}\\
F-INS-1 & Selectable antenna types: horn, parabolic dish, Yagi; further
  types as subclasses. & 2 & open \\
F-INS-2 & Receiver model with the device profile of the Ettus USRP B210
  (two coherent channels). & 2 & open \\
F-INS-3 & Stations with geodetic position (WGS84) and an antenna;
  arrays of two or more stations; baselines. & 2 & open \\
F-INS-4 & Antennas, receivers and sites are configured in files
  (\texttt{configs/}). & 1 & done \\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Observation}}\\
F-OBS-1 & Hour angle, elevation and uv-coverage with Earth rotation
  synthesis (sidereal and solar rate). & 2 & open \\
F-OBS-2 & Direct computation of visibilities with noise (radiometer
  equation, SEFD). & 2 & open \\
F-OBS-3 & Transit (drift scan) of fixed antennas as time series
  (\texttt{TransitSimulator}). & 2 & open \\
F-OBS-4 & Adding and multiplying interferometer; fringe rate and
  delay. & 2 & open \\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Signal (closed simulation)}}\\
F-SIG-1 & Simulated IQ samples per station with geometric delay, fringe
  phase, primary beam attenuation and receiver noise. & 2 & open \\
F-SIG-2 & FX correlator: fractional delay, fringe stopping, delay/rate
  search, cross spectra $\rightarrow$ \texttt{VisData}. & 2 & open \\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Imaging and deconvolution}}\\
F-IMG-1 & Gridding; natural, uniform and Wiener weighting; dirty image
  and dirty beam, per channel or channel average. & 3 & open \\
F-DEC-1 & Högbom CLEAN with clean beam and restoration. & 3 & open \\
F-DEC-2 & Wiener filter for filled apertures. & 3 & open \\
F-DEC-3 & Comparison metrics (effective beam width, RMS error against a
  reference). & 3 & open \\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Data and user interface}}\\
F-DAT-1 & One visibility data class (\texttt{VisData}) with frequency
  axis for simulated and measured data. & 2 & open \\
F-DAT-2 & FITS input/output compatible with \texttt{aas6sim}
  (simulated data marked by \texttt{SIMULATD = T}). & 2 & open \\
F-UI-1 & Interactive notebooks (ipywidgets), one per topic; usable
  without programming knowledge. & 4 & open \\
F-UI-2 & Notebooks~A (Wiener filter vs.\ CLEAN) and~B (the Sun as a
  first target) reproduced with the package. & 4 & open \\
\end{longtable}
