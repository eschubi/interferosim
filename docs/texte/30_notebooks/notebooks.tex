ipywidgets controls, interactive plots, one notebook per topic
(\texttt{notebooks/}). Each notebook ends with a selector for the
interactive use; static figures show the results also without a running
kernel.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{9.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
Notebook & Content \\
\midrule
\texttt{00\_general} & project overview: goal of a VLB interferometer
  with an 11.5\,km baseline, principle, key figures, clock and timing
  requirements, sensitivity, road map \\
\texttt{01\_sky\_models} & sky models, file format, maps, own skies,
  visibilities, catalogue of known regions with selector and gallery \\
\texttt{02\_instrument} & antenna types and primary beams, transfer
  function of the dish, B210 and sensitivity (S/N of the sky models on
  both arrays), arrays and baselines, selector \\
\texttt{03\_signal\_simulation} & SDR receiver signals (IQ) from the sky
  model under variable baseline parameters: signal model, raw fringes,
  measured vs.\ model visibilities over the baseline length, clock errors,
  quantisation, SigMF output, selector \\
\texttt{04\_observation} & sources over the day, delay and fringe rate,
  uv-coverage (Earth rotation synthesis), visibilities over a track,
  transit of the Sun with two fixed horns, asymmetry of a track, selector \\
\texttt{05\_correlator} & FX correlator on simulated streams with clock
  and LO offsets: cross spectrum and lag spectrum, delay--rate search and
  refinement, cross spectra after the solution, channels and noise,
  selector \\
\texttt{A\_fringes} & theory A (first version): fringe pattern on the
  sky, fringe rate from the Earth's rotation, fringe contrast and source
  size, fringes in a long track (Cygnus~A with zooms of 5--20\,min,
  natural and structure fringe rate), selector \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
